# V1 business workflows for React

These are navigation and mutation sequences. Exact inputs/outputs/permissions are in [controller contracts](api/), [DTOs](DTO_AND_ENUM_REFERENCE.md), [OpenAPI](openapi.integration.json), and the linked backend feature docs. All examples use fabricated identifiers. Preserve stable operation IDs for exact retries; refresh authoritative state after financial mutations.

## Identity, members and normal gym use

Login, then load `/api/auth/me` and feature catalogs. Search members and leads with the documented bounded pages. Registration, Lead conversion and reassignment retain historical commercial Sales ownership. A Manager can also be a personally assigned Sales employee on the same account. Member registration, paid Sale/renewal and collections require the actual employee's eligible open Shift; login does not auto-open one. New/Renewal commission attribution follows assigned owner collection evidence, not the cashier or whole branch revenue.

Sell via `POST /api/sales`, inspect the returned Sale ID with `GET /api/sales/{id}`, and collect later through `POST /api/sales/{id}/payments` where applicable. Use actual branch Cash/InstaPay financial account IDs and payment parts; optional `providerReference` on InstaPay payment/refund parts may be null or empty. A split collection is one commercial payment with actual linked ledger parts, not separate sale revenue. Check-in eligibility should be previewed then registered explicitly; a response may be HTTP 200 with `allowed:false`. Reception check-in never consumes Private sessions.

Reception with `Sales.QuickCancel`, Reception membership, own Shift and branch scope may use `POST /api/sales/{id}/quick-cancel` within **24 actual hours inclusive** from persisted purchase time. The body explicitly chooses zero, partial or full refund. No automated usage deduction applies to Gym, Private or Classes. After that, an authorized BranchManager/Owner uses `POST /api/sales/{id}/refunds` with `Payments.Refund` and Sale branch scope; a Manager needs their own Shift, while the Owner administrative path is shift-free. Positive refunds cannot exceed net collected money and need outbound account parts summing exactly; zero cancellation has no ledger movement. Finalized commission/closing restrictions remain. Historical attendance and attribution remain. See [Sales contract](api/Sales.md).

## Shift, drawer, expenses and month

Employees explicitly open their own Shift with `POST /api/shifts`. Before closing, show `GET /api/shifts/{id}/summary`: the operator's own linked activity is distinct from **all branch activity during that Shift interval**, including overlapping coworkers and Owner entries. Page own ledger rows at `/{id}/transactions` and branch-period rows at `/{id}/branch-transactions`; both require `Shifts.View` **and** `Vault.View`. Branch-period totals cover the whole interval, not only a page, and account rows reconcile opening + inflows − outflows to expected book balances. Cash and InstaPay are separate. Current shared balances repeated across overlapping Shifts must never be added together.

Self-closure uses `POST /api/shifts/{id}/close` and requires a real physical cash count. An authorized Owner/BranchManager may use `/{id}/close-override` for someone else's Shift in scope, with reason/version. Omitted physical count yields `Unverified`, null counted cash and null discrepancy; it is still a closed Shift and does not post money. Closed Shift history uses closure cutoff/snapshot; live shared balances remain live. Legacy timestamp limitations are reported in `branchPeriod.limitations`. Owner administrative Cash/InstaPay expenses use `POST /api/expenses` without an Owner Shift; the actual Owner user is recorded and the expense appears in branch-period activity. Other employees need their own Shift. See [Shift contract](api/Shifts.md).

Financial months are branch-specific. `POST /api/financial-months` opens explicit BusinessDate bounds; close requires every branch Shift closed and the end BusinessDate reached. Optional final cash count does not block on discrepancy. `POST /{id}/close` freezes JSON and six-sheet XLSX revision; `POST /{id}/reopen` records audit history without silently changing finalized Sales/Coach statements. Query report, transactions, revisions and events; `GET /{id}/export?revision=` downloads the frozen workbook with `X-Workbook-SHA256`. Current-period transaction details exclude prior months; carried-forward balances use historical aggregates. Late discovered postings go to a next open financial month with separately recorded original occurrence when known. See [Monthly Finance contract](api/MonthlyFinance.md).

## Earnings and planning

Independent New/Renewal Sales targets, source collections, progress and finalized statements are exposed by [Commissions](api/Commissions.md). Salary settings/history are in [EmployeeSalaries](api/EmployeeSalaries.md); monthly branch salary preview and authorized partial employee payment records are in [MonthlyFinance](api/MonthlyFinance.md) and [Payroll](api/Payroll.md). Payroll payment records do **not** post cash ledger rows or identify funding account; do not also enter them as actual Expenses. Coach earnings and actual partial payouts use the existing separate Coaching finance workflow. Private registration requires a valid gym membership and same-BusinessDate reception check-in; Classes registration requires neither and draws one shared Classes session across class types. Incoming transferred-package Private coach gets fixed 50%; actual delivered and Owner-expiry-recognized units remain distinct. Classes compensation remains disabled. See [Private/Classes rules](PRIVATE_CLASSES.md).

`POST /api/maintenance-plans` appends an Owner-controlled plan revision; list, summary and reminders are informational. A 15,000 EGP plan every 3 months suggests 5,000/month but creates no reserve balance, Expense or ledger movement. A real payment uses `POST /api/expenses` once. Plan due dates are reminders, not automatic recurrence/payment proof. See [planning contract](PLANNED_PERIODIC_EXPENSES.md).
